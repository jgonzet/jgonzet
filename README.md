# Hi, I'm José 👋

I'm a data scientist who likes building things that actually work outside a notebook.

I spent 7+ years applying ML to physical systems: first detecting faults in drones, then modeling steel mills and hot rolling at Ternium, where I ended up leading a regional AI team across LATAM. Now I'm back to building with my own hands: LLM apps, evaluation and AI engineering.

## What I'm into

- **Industrial data science:** process models, hybrid physics + ML, virtual sensors, time-series forecasting
- **LLM applications:** tools that give grounded answers and can prove it with evaluation
- **Shipping:** taking an idea from "what if…" to something people can use

## Things I'm building

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Manager Assistant](https://github.com/jgonzet/kalia) | A work assistant for managers. It pulls commitments out of emails, meetings and chats so nothing falls through the cracks, and answers questions grounded in that context. Every stage is evaluated. |
| [ETF Portfolio Analyzer](https://github.com/jgonzet/etf-portfolio-analyzer) | Owning five ETFs doesn't mean you're diversified. This shows your real sector exposure and overlap, with LLM-generated analysis on top. FastAPI, yfinance, typed data pipeline. |
